Wireless communication flow

1
Soil sensor units

Each row measures its own soil moisture and sends the reading from its ESP32.

2
Central controller

Receives both readings and compares each value with its configured threshold.

3
Valve units

Each valve ESP32 receives its row-specific ON/OFF command and controls the relay.

4
Automatic cycle

Dry soil opens its valve; reaching the target moisture closes it.

Demo controls

These controls demonstrate the dashboard only. They do not operate physical valves unless the backend is connected to your ESP32 hardware.
